Likely Attack Narrative

The most defensible sequence is a hybrid compromise: an external operator established or used a covert HTTPS channel from jump-station5, then used the same environment to perform service-based lateral movement and reconnaissance. The adam_admin identity was then abused across cloud services, enabling mailbox persistence, OAuth-based persistence, AWS access-key creation, Azure privilege changes, secret retrieval, and diagnostic logging impairment. The clustering of activities within roughly three hours, across network, M365, Entra ID, AWS, and Azure, makes benign administrative activity unlikely.

Recommended Executive Actions
